# Usability testing — South View map

Use this with **2 testers** before the deadline. Record their feedback.

## Tasks to run

1. **Search**  
   “Search for someone by name.”  
   - Do they use the name box? Do results update as they type?

2. **Filter**  
   “Find burials in a specific section” or “Filter by funeral home.”  
   - Do they use the Section / Funeral home dropdowns? Do the map and list update?

3. **Detail**  
   “Open a burial’s detail card.”  
   - Do they click a marker or a list item? Can they read dates, section, funeral home?

4. **Map**  
   “Navigate to a grave on the map.”  
   - Do they use the map after opening a detail? Does “List view” help?

5. **Guided tour**  
   “Try the guided tour.”  
   - Do they find “Start Guided Tour”? Do Prev/Next and the path make sense?

## What to record

- Where they hesitate or get stuck.
- What they expect (e.g. “I expected search to also search by date”).
- What they like (e.g. list view, related burials).
- Any bugs (wrong data, broken filters, map not loading).

## After testing

- Fix critical bugs first.
- Note “Phase 2” ideas (e.g. admin login) for the presentation, not for this build.
