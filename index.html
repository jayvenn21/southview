Figures Connected to South-View

Martin Luther King Sr.Funeral held at South-View Cemetery. Buried at Lincoln Cemetery, Atlanta.
John Wesley DobbsKnown as the "unofficial mayor of Auburn Avenue." Civic leader tied to the South-View community.
Alonzo HerndonFounder of Atlanta Life Insurance. Buried at South-View; his legacy shaped Black Atlanta's economy.
Antoine GravesOne of the original founders of South-View Cemetery in 1886, ensuring Black families had a dignified place of rest.
Dr. Henry Rutherford ButlerPhysician and civil rights leader. Among the early advocates for Black healthcare in Atlanta.

How to read the map

  • Brown dots — individual burial locations with known coordinates.
  • Numbered circles — clusters of nearby graves. Click to zoom in and expand.
  • Rose-colored dots — graves that share coordinates with others (exact plot location unknown).
  • Gold dot with white halo — the burial you currently have selected.
